Farming Roadmap

Monthly Guide.

A comprehensive 12-month strategy for maximizing your agricultural output.

January
Rabi
  • Irrigate Wheat and Barley crops.
  • Apply second dose of urea if needed.
  • Protect crops from frost (sheet covering).
01
February
Rabi
  • Harvest early Potato & Mustard.
  • Prepare land for Sugarcane planting.
  • Start sowing Cucurbits (Melons).
02
March
Zaid
  • Harvest Wheat, Gram, and Barley.
  • Sow Zaid crops (Moong, Watermelon).
  • Deep ploughing for summer solarization.
03
April
Zaid
  • Threshing and storage of Rabi produce.
  • Irrigate Zaid vegetables and pulses.
  • Apply manure to prepared fields.
04
May
Zaid
  • Deep summer ploughing to kill pests.
  • Procure seeds for Kharif season.
  • Prepare nurseries for Paddy (Rice).
05
June
Kharif
  • Sow Cotton, Maize, and Jowar.
  • Transplant Paddy seedlings.
  • Green manuring (Dhaincha).
06
July
Kharif
  • Continue Paddy transplantation.
  • Weeding in Cotton and Maize.
  • Plantation of fruit trees.
07
August
Kharif
  • Top dressing of Urea in Paddy.
  • Pest control (Stem borer/Leaf folder).
  • Harvest early pulses.
08
September
Kharif
  • Harvest early Maize and Jowar.
  • Prepare land for Mustard.
  • Stop irrigation in Paddy before harvest.
09
October
Rabi
  • Harvest Paddy and Cotton.
  • Sow Mustard, Gram, Peas, and Lentil.
  • Sow autumnal Sugarcane.
10
November
Rabi
  • Sow Wheat and Barley (Optimal).
  • First irrigation in Mustard.
  • Weeding in early sown Rabi crops.
11
December
Rabi
  • First irrigation in Wheat.
  • Late sowing of Wheat (if delayed).
  • Protect crops from cold waves.
12
