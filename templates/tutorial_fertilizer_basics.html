🧪 Fertilizer Basics

What are Fertilizers?

Fertilizers provide essential nutrients to plants that help in healthy growth and better yield.

Main Nutrients (NPK)

  • Nitrogen (N): Leaf growth
  • Phosphorus (P): Root development
  • Potassium (K): Disease resistance

Types of Fertilizers

  • Organic fertilizers (compost, manure)
  • Chemical fertilizers (urea, DAP)
  • Bio-fertilizers

Safe Usage Tips

  • Follow recommended dosage
  • Avoid overuse
  • Apply during proper crop stage
