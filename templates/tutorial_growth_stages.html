🌿 Crop Growth Stages

1️⃣ Germination Stage

This is the first stage where the seed absorbs water, swells, and starts sprouting.

2️⃣ Vegetative Stage

The plant develops roots, stems, and leaves. Proper water and nutrients are crucial in this stage.

3️⃣ Flowering Stage

Flowers appear and pollination occurs. Stress during this stage can reduce yield.

4️⃣ Fruiting / Grain Filling Stage

Fruits or grains develop and increase in size. Balanced nutrition is important here.

5️⃣ Maturity & Harvesting Stage

The crop reaches maturity and is ready for harvesting. Timely harvest prevents losses.
