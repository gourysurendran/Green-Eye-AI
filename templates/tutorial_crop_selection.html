🌿 Crop Selection

Why Crop Selection is Important

Choosing the right crop helps reduce losses, improves yield, and increases profit.

Factors to Consider

  • Soil type
  • Season (Kharif / Rabi / Zaid)
  • Water availability
  • Climate and temperature

Examples

  • Rice → Clay / Loamy soil, high water
  • Wheat → Loamy soil, moderate water
  • Millets → Dry regions, low water
