🐛 Pest & Disease Awareness

Why Pest Control is Important

Pests and diseases can reduce crop yield and quality. Early identification helps prevent major losses.

Common Crop Pests

  • Aphids
  • Whiteflies
  • Caterpillars
  • Stem borers

Common Plant Diseases

  • Leaf blight
  • Powdery mildew
  • Root rot
  • Wilt disease

Basic Prevention Methods

  • Use disease-resistant varieties
  • Maintain field hygiene
  • Rotate crops
  • Monitor crops regularly
