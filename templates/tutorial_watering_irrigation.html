💧 Watering & Irrigation

Why Water is Important

Water is essential for plant growth, nutrient absorption, and maintaining soil moisture.

When to Water Crops

  • Early morning or late evening
  • Avoid watering during hot afternoons
  • Water based on crop stage

Common Irrigation Methods

  • Flood irrigation
  • Drip irrigation (water-efficient)
  • Sprinkler irrigation

Watering Tips

  • Avoid over-watering
  • Ensure proper drainage
  • Use mulching to retain moisture
