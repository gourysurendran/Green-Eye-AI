Agricultural Cycles

Sowing Seasons.

Plan your year with the traditional Indian agricultural calendar, optimized for modern yields.

June – October

Kharif Season

Monsoon crops that thrive in high temperature and high moisture conditions.

  • Rice (Paddy)
  • Maize & Sorghum
  • Cotton & Jute
  • Soybean & Groundnut
  • Sugarcane
October – March

Rabi Season

Winter crops sown after the monsoon rains have subsided, requiring cool climates.

  • Wheat & Barley
  • Mustard & Rapeseed
  • Peas & Gram
  • Potato & Tomato
  • Onions
March – June

Zaid Season

Summer crops grown on irrigated lands during the short gap between Rabi and Kharif.

  • Watermelon & Muskmelon
  • Cucumber & Bitter Gourd
  • Pumpkin
  • Sunflower
  • Seasonal Vegetables
