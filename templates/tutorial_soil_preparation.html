Soil Preparation

The foundation of a good harvest lies in how well you prepare the soil.

Why is Soil Preparation Important?

Soil preparation ensures that the soil is loose, fertile, and free of weeds. It helps roots penetrate deep into the ground and absorb nutrients effectively.

Steps for Soil Preparation:

  1. Poughing (Tilling): Loosening and turning the soil to bring nutrients to the top.
  2. Leveling: Ensuring the land is even to prevent water logging or soil erosion.
  3. Manuring: Adding organic manure or fertilizers to enrich the soil before sowing.
