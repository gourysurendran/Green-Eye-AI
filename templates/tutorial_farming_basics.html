Farming Basics

An introduction to the world of agriculture for new farmers.

What is Farming?

Farming is the practice of growing harvestable crops and raising animals for food, fiber, medicinal plants, and other products used to sustain and enhance human life.

Types of Farming

  • Subsistence: Growing purely for self-consumption.
  • Commercial: Large-scale production for selling.
  • Organic: Farming without synthetic chemicals.

Key Terminology

  • Crop: A plant cultivated for harvest.
  • Soil: The upper layer of earth in which plants grow.
  • Irrigation: Artificial application of water.
  • Harvest: Process of gathering mature crops.
