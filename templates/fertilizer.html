How to find these values?

Agri Soil Test Lab

Visit your nearest government or private soil testing laboratory. This is the most accurate way.

Home Testing Kits

Buy a portable NPK test kit from an agricultural store. It uses color cards to show nutrient levels.

Digital Moisture Meter

Use a handheld moisture sensor to get instant percentage readings from your soil.

Soil Texture Check

The easiest way to check your soil is the "Squeeze Test". Dampen a handful of soil and squeeze it firmly.

Gritty & Loose (Sandy)

If it feels gritty and doesn't form a ball (falls apart), it is Sandy soil.

Soft & Crumbly (Loam)

If it forms a ball that crumbles when poked, it is Loamy. Best for most crops.

Sticky & Firm (Clay)

If it forms a tight, sticky ball that doesn't break easily, it is Clay soil.
