Seed Selection & Sowing

The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step, or in farming, a single seed.

Importance of Quality

High-quality seeds are the catalyst for a successful harvest. They ensure:

  • Better germination rates (90%+)
  • Stronger immunity against early diseases
  • Uniform plant growth and higher yield

Selection Checklist

  • Certified Seeds: Always prefer HYV (High Yielding Variety) certified by agencies.
  • Moisture Content: Seeds should be dry (10-12% moisture).
  • Physical Purity: Avoid broken, shriveled, or weed-infested seeds.

Sowing Methods

Broadcasting:
Scattering seeds by hand. Fast but uneven.
Dibbling:
Placing seeds in holes at fixed intervals. Precise but labor-intensive.
Drilling:
Using seed drills for uniform depth and spacing. Highly recommended.

Rules of Thumb for Sowing:

  • Depth: Usually 2-3 times the size of the seed. Too deep = no germination. Too shallow = bird attack.
  • Spacing: Give each plant room to breathe and access sunlight.
  • Moisture: Soil must be moist at the time of sowing.
