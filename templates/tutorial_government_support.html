🏛️ Government Support for Farmers

PM-KISAN Scheme

Provides income support of ₹6000 per year to eligible farmers in three equal installments.

Crop Insurance (PMFBY)

Protects farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Offers affordable credit for cultivation and agricultural expenses.

Subsidies & Support

  • Seed and fertilizer subsidies
  • Irrigation equipment support
  • Soil testing assistance
