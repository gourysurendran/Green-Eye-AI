🧺 Harvesting & Storage

When to Harvest

Harvest crops at the right maturity stage to get better quality and maximum yield.

Signs of Crop Maturity

  • Change in color of grains or fruits
  • Drying of leaves or stems
  • Hardness of seeds or grains

Harvesting Methods

  • Manual harvesting (hand tools)
  • Mechanical harvesting (harvesters)

Proper Storage Practices

  • Dry crops properly before storage
  • Use clean and dry storage containers
  • Protect from pests and moisture
  • Store in well-ventilated areas
