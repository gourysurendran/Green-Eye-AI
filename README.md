# 🌿 Green Eye – Where Nature Meets Intelligence

> **An AI-Driven Integrated Farmer Guidance System**

Green Eye is an intelligent agricultural assistance platform that leverages Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to help farmers make informed decisions. The system combines plant disease detection, crop recommendations, irrigation guidance, fertilizer suggestions, market insights, and government scheme information into a single user-friendly platform.

# 📌 Project Overview

Agriculture plays a vital role in food production, but farmers often face challenges such as plant diseases, improper irrigation, unsuitable crop selection, and limited access to timely agricultural information.

**Green Eye** addresses these problems by integrating AI models with agricultural knowledge to provide accurate, intelligent, and easy-to-understand recommendations.

The goal is to improve productivity, reduce crop losses, and support sustainable farming practices.

# 🚀 How It Works

1. Upload a plant leaf image.
2. The CNN model processes the image.
3. The system predicts the plant disease.
4. Green Eye generates:

   * Disease name
   * Confidence score
   * Symptoms
   * Recommended treatment
   * Preventive measures
5. Farmers can also access:

   * Crop recommendations
   * Fertilizer guidance
   * Irrigation advice
   * Market prices
   * Government schemes
   * AI chatbot assistance
